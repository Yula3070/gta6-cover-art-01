Vice Coast Cover Studio — an offline web app that turns your photos and a caption into cover art in the style of GTA 6's posters: multi-panel layouts, bold lettering, a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade, exported as a PNG to share. Your photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — add a photo (or try a sample photo), pick a layout, click a panel to crop, pick a grade, edit the title and subtitle, choose a size, and export PNG.
